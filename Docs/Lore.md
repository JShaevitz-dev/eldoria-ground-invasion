# Ground Invasion: Setting and Lore

A space opera whose tone sits between Star Trek and Warhammer 40k. It replaces the WWII-inspired fantasy setting of the
original Eldoria. The game rules live in [Rules.md](Rules.md); this document is the setting they serve.

# **The Known Races of the Setting**

The galaxy contains several distinct sapient civilizations, each with its own biology, culture, political institutions,
and relationship with the Free Nations Alliance. Some are full members, some are associate members, and others remain
outside the Alliance or are its adversaries.

This overview covers their established characteristics, with additional detail on the Kobolds and Dragonborn of
Draconis Imperialis.

## **1. Humanity**

**Political status:** Founding member and leading military power of the Free Nations Alliance.

**Homeworld:** Terra (Earth).

**Government:** Multiple sovereign liberal democracies cooperating through the Alliance.

Humanity is the Alliance's principal military power and its Shield Bearer.

**Biological characteristics**

- Exceptional stamina and endurance.
- High environmental adaptability.
- Strong capacity for sustained physical activity.
- Relatively unremarkable absolute strength compared with some other species.
- Ability to sustain long infantry marches, giving human forces significant logistical advantages.

**Cultural and political characteristics**

- Strong emphasis on individual liberty, universal rights, and democratic governance.
- Humanity played a major role in establishing the Alliance's legal framework.
- Human genetic engineering law prohibits modifying whole organisms beyond traditional domestication and selective
  breeding. Engineered cloned organs are permitted for medical purposes.
- Human space has become a haven for minorities and emigrants from less accommodating civilizations.

**Military distinction:** The ISF is humanity's unified armed service, not the military of the entire Alliance. Its
doctrine emphasizes Supremacy: rapid application of overwhelming force, adaptability, and broad-spectrum capability.

## **2. The Skael**

**Political status:** Full Alliance member.

**Biology:** Small, rodent-primate-like species, approximately half human height.

The Skael are an arboreal species whose evolutionary history profoundly influences their modern civilization.

**Biological characteristics**

- One biological sex and three genders: Gold, Silver, and Bronze.
- Extremely dexterous feet, nearly comparable to their hands.
- True rotating ankles and strong grasping ability.
- Excellent climbing ability.
- Can tolerate being upside down indefinitely.
- More resistant than humans to muscular deconditioning associated with zero gravity.
- Lower absolute muscle strength than humans.

**Gender and household structure**

Traditional Skael households are polyamorous and organized around three genders:

| Gender | Traditional role                            |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------- |
| Gold   | Masculine household leader                  |
| Silver | Androgynous defenders and workers           |
| Bronze | Feminine domestic managers and childbearers |

These are traditional arrangements, not universal rules governing every household.

**Cultural characteristics**

- Predominantly nudist, with jewelry and accessories communicating gender, rank, and social role.
- Traditionally sleep in communal piles, often holding or grasping something while sleeping.
- Military ships preserve communal sleeping arrangements, often separated by rank.
- Ladders are preferred over stairs in many buildings.
- Machine interfaces frequently use grasping foot controls rather than human-style pedals.

**Military distinction:** Skael prohibit psionics from military service. Their starships also have distinctive
architecture built around a central multipurpose command center, with functional sections branching outward.

## **3. The Chrome**

**Political status:** Full Alliance member.

The Chrome have a deeply mathematical religious worldview. They worship mathematical constants as evidence that their
gods impose structure upon reality.

**Cultural characteristics**

- Mathematics is treated as evidence of divine authorship.
- Their religious beliefs emphasize the underlying order of creation.
- Coming of age involves extracting the brain and placing it into a robotic shell.
- Their robotic bodies represent a religious transition into adulthood.

**First contact**

Humanity's first contact with the Chrome occurred through the ISF *Providence* and the Chrome vessel M-55567.

The human transmission included π and the Pythagorean theorem. The Chrome crew were overjoyed: humanity had
independently recognized mathematical truths they considered sacred.

The encounter became an important cultural and diplomatic milestone. The Chrome embassy in New York even has a mural
depicting the cover of the voyage record.

**Strategic distinction:** The Chrome possess a planetary weapon capable of using nanotechnology to kill animal life
across an entire planet and resurrect it as zombies.

**On the battlefield (The Chrome Techno-democracy, planned):** the Chrome have no standard soldier: each Chrome's body
is built for its purpose, so every Chrome unit is unique. What they share is an aesthetic of smooth lines and insectoid
shapes. Like the game's other machines, Chrome units trend towards high Armor rather than large Health pools.

## **4. The Rask**

**Political status:** Associate member.

**Biology:** Rodent-like.

The Rask are a highly ritualistic, collectivist civilization governed by a theocratic communist state.

**Religion and government**

- Rigid patriarchal monotheism.
- A single church oversees the totalitarian government.
- Scripture dictates social and political conduct.
- Rask religious belief includes a conviction of their own moral and material superiority.
- They generally regard Alliance civilizations as backwards.

Their modern theocratic communism has an unusual historical origin. During the industrial era, mass printing enabled
widespread translation of religious texts. A linguistic shift changed the description of God from one who *creates*
the universe to one who *crafts* it. The resulting image of God as a tradesman contributed to the civilization's
ideological transformation.

**Language**

- The original Rask language has no grammatical plurals.
- Quantity is expressed through separate quantifiers.
- For example, the equivalent of "three apples" is "three apple."
- Modern Rask speakers sometimes use pluralized forms through exposure to other languages.
- Holy Rask-speak retains the original grammatical rule; pluralizing a noun is a serious breach.

**Political relationship with humanity**

The Human–Rask War began over competing claims to Canvas. The Rask flagship was destroyed by the ISF destroyer
*Louverture* in orbit around the Rask homeworld.

The subsequent treaty granted the Rask associate membership in exchange for:

1. Renouncing their claim to Canvas.
2. Permitting regulated migration between Rask territory and Alliance territories.
3. Paying reparations.
4. Guaranteeing protection for all Alliance races.

The Rask remain culturally isolated and maintain minimal trade with the Alliance.

## **5. The Sphinxians**

**Political status:** Full Alliance member.

**Homeworld:** T'dae.

**Biology:** All-female, hairless feline-like species.

The Sphinxians are an all-female civilization whose modern reproductive biology and religious culture emerged from a
catastrophic pandemic during their atomic age.

**Biological and reproductive history**

- A pandemic destroyed a hormone-regulating organ, lungs, and hearts.
- The loss of the hormone-regulating organ was manageable for females through hormone replacement therapy.
- Males were disproportionately affected, resulting in a catastrophic population decline.
- Sphinxians eventually developed egg fusion and bred out males within a generation.

**Cultural characteristics**

- Originally egalitarian, their society and religious traditions gradually became matriarchal.
- Their modern faith regards the ability to bear children as a divine gift from a goddess.
- Obstetric care incorporates religious ceremony.
- Maternity Priestesses complete medical and theological education, with senior members specializing in delivery and
  midwifery.
- Their reproductive legislation is extensive because of their reliance on egg fusion and genetic screening.

**Language**

- Masculine pronouns are interchangeable with inanimate pronouns.
- Male aliens may consequently be referred to using an equivalent of "it."
- Some regional dialects have abandoned the inanimate pronoun, using feminine pronouns for living beings and masculine
  pronouns for objects.

**Psionics and military distinction**

All Sphinxians are innately psionic, although individual strength varies.

Their planetary weapon uses overwhelming psionic power to induce amnesia and seizures throughout an entire population.

## **6. The Hive**

**Political status:** Associate nation.

**Biology:** Insectoid, six-legged, humanoid upper body, innately psionic.

The Hive are a civilization organized around a psionic hivemind.

**Biological and cognitive characteristics**

- Individual members possess personalities and some autonomy.
- Their minds are interconnected, so they are never entirely separate from the collective.
- They are fully sentient and sapient.
- Individualist thought, as understood by humans, is fundamentally alien to them.

**Political characteristics**

- They attempted to expand into Skael territory, prompting an Alliance military response.
- Following the war, they became an associate nation.
- Their brain caste identified diplomatic resolution as the path forward.
- They have no ambition to become a liberal democracy or full Alliance member.
- They prefer some separation from individualist civilizations.

**Military relationship**

The Hive are not formally at war with Draconis Imperialis, but have made an informal pledge to support the Alliance
militarily if hostilities resume.

Their associate status is a deliberate political choice, not a transitional step toward full membership.

## **7. Draconis Imperialis**

**Political status:** Hostile power under an indefinite cease-fire with the Free Nations Alliance.

**Extent:** Approximately one thousand worlds.

**Biology:** Large, quadrupedal, winged, pseudo-endothermic reptiles.

Draconis Imperialis is an ancient empire and the Alliance's principal historical adversary.

### **The Dragons**

The Dragons are the Imperial ruling caste.

**Physical characteristics**

- Approximately twice the size of a Shire horse.
- Quadrupedal, winged reptiles capable of flight.
- Forelimbs have rotating wrists, three articulated fingers, and an articulated thumb.
- Every Dragon descends from a genetically engineered template of physical perfection.
- Their genetic rigidity prevents the development of psionics.

**Political characteristics**

- Governed by an absolute monarchy.
- Organized into great houses, each possessing its own territory and military.
- The Imperial House provides central authority over the Empire.
- Dragons are not merely aristocratic administrators; they will personally enter combat when circumstances demand it.

### **The Kobolds**

Kobolds are an artificially bred, bipedal reptilian species created by the Dragons to serve as menials and household
servants.

**Physical and biological characteristics**

- Approximately half human height.
- Reptilian appearance.
- Bipedal locomotion.
- Artificially bred by the Dragons.
- Unable to develop psionics.

**Cognitive characteristics**

- Fully sapient and capable of independent thought and action.
- Highly intelligent despite their socially subordinate position.
- Their submissive disposition is the result of breeding, upbringing, and prolonged social conditioning rather than an
  absence of intelligence or free will.

**Social role**

- Traditionally employed as household servants, attendants, and menial workers.
- Raised within a system that conditions them to serve Dragons.
- Their status under Imperial law is that of an enslaved population.

**Military applications**

The Empire also uses Kobolds in military robotics.

Rather than trusting artificial intelligence to operate independently, the Empire frequently places a Kobold pilot
inside robotic platforms. The Kobold is considered as expendable as an AI core.

One example is the Empire's small, unarmed reconnaissance aircraft. These aircraft sometimes get shot down only for the
recovering force to discover:

- A single Kobold pilot.
- A pistol carried for personal defense.
- A flight computer configured to erase itself after mission completion.

This illustrates the Empire's preference for biological intelligence over autonomous artificial intelligence, even
when the biological operator is treated as disposable.

**Treatment in human custody**

The ISF treats captured Kobolds as prisoners of war. They are not considered worthwhile ransom or interrogation
targets. They receive medical treatment, are cataloged, and are sent to the POW camp on Pyre.

Once acclimated to captivity, many become docile and sociable. The Pyre facility consequently operates at minimum
security, with simple work provided to occupy prisoners.

Human and UN doctrine prohibits releasing a sapient being from human custody into bondage. Their psychological
conditioning complicates questions of voluntary repatriation and eventual integration into human society.

### **The Dragonborn**

Dragonborn are an artificially bred, bipedal winged reptilian species created by the Dragons. They serve as the
principal military and manual-labor caste of Draconis Imperialis.

**Physical and biological characteristics**

- Approximately one and a half times human height.
- Bipedal, with wings.
- Reptilian anatomy.
- Exceptionally powerful musculature.
- Their physical strength can outperform the servos of human Aegis powered armor.
- Unable to develop psionics.

**Cognitive and social characteristics**

- Fully sapient.
- Highly disciplined and rigidly organized.
- Their social conditioning and training reinforce their assigned place in Imperial society.
- They are not unintelligent or incapable of independent thought.

**Military role**

Dragonborn form the bulk of the Draconis Imperialis Army.

Their military conditioning is extraordinary:

- They can become battlefield-capable only five years after hatching.
- They are trained to operate within highly disciplined formations.
- Their physical strength makes them formidable infantry and laborers.
- They are employed extensively in military and industrial work.

Unlike the Kobolds, whose primary traditional role is domestic and menial service, Dragonborn are bred specifically for
a combination of military service and heavy manual labor.

**Treatment in human custody**

Captured Dragonborn are treated under the same basic POW doctrine as Kobolds. They are transferred to Pyre after
treatment and cataloging.

The ISF's opposition to slavery means they cannot simply be returned to the Empire. Their possible integration into
human society remains a subject of debate.

### **Draconis Imperial military doctrine**

The Empire's military doctrine is the ideological opposite of ISF Supremacy.

- Purpose-built formations and equipment.
- Extensive specialization.
- Centralized control.
- Technological superiority.
- Willingness to abandon tradition and convention.
- Acceptance of enormous casualties when necessary to achieve strategic objectives.
- Use of disposable cloned soldiers and workers.

The Empire distrusts artificial intelligence and prefers biological intelligence under Imperial control. It would
rather breed a smarter Kobold than develop an autonomous AI.

### **Political situation**

The Empire and the Alliance fought to an indefinite cease-fire. Neither side achieved its ultimate objectives.

Within the Alliance, there is an ongoing disagreement over whether peace is possible with the Empire intact or whether
regime change or complete dissolution is necessary.

Ka'dira, a former patriarch of a great house, defected to human space and provided valuable intelligence, translation
technology, and intact military equipment in exchange for asylum and an exceptional settlement.

## **The larger political picture**

The Alliance does not require every civilization to become culturally or politically identical.

Three broad categories emerge:

- **Full members:** Liberal democracies that meet the Alliance's legal and institutional requirements.
- **Associate members:** Civilizations that maintain treaty relationships without meeting every requirement for full
  membership or without seeking it.
- **External powers:** Civilizations such as Draconis Imperialis, whose political and military interests remain in
  conflict with the Alliance.

The central tension is that the Alliance seeks to defend universal rights while respecting the sovereignty and cultural
independence of its members. Its relationship with Draconis Imperialis tests how far that principle can extend when
confronted with a civilization whose domestic institutions depend upon the subjugation of other sapient populations.
